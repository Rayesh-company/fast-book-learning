# Segment sizing and reachability: Iran's depth-dissatisfied Farsi self-improvers

- **Ticket:** [aubed9/chat-with-books#42](https://github.com/aubed9/chat-with-books/issues/42) (research, blocks #41 business model)
- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Question:** How large and reachable is the depth-dissatisfied Farsi self-improver pool in Iran — and where does it gather? How does it discover and pay for learning products?
- **Method:** Web research (Farsi + English), official pages fetched where reachable, news/wire sources otherwise. Every claim carries a numbered source ([S#]); facts are marked **[F]**, hypotheses/arithmetic/interpretations **[H]**. Self-reported platform numbers are labeled as such. No invented figures; where a number could not be sourced, the gap is stated.

**Bottom line:** No direct measurement of the *depth-dissatisfied* fraction exists anywhere — that number must come from the exit-criterion interviews. What the proxies bound: the broad Farsi digital-reading audience is in the low millions of monthly actives (self-reported platform numbers: Taaghche 15M installs, Fidibo 10M registered, ~3M monthly book-consuming users market-wide), engaged *religious* readers — the beachhead book's natural audience — show up at 1M+ participants/year in a single reading festival and a ~340,000-circle institutional study-circle network, and the beachhead book's own text and audio are distributed **free** by the author's official institute. They gather on Telegram/Eitaa book channels, Instagram book pages, BPLUS-scale summary podcasts, mosque study circles, the Razavi book-reading festival, and the Tehran Intl Book Fair. Payment is SHETAB-only domestic rails through Cafe Bazaar/Myket in-app billing or web gateways; pricing anchors run from 28k Toman/month (Microketab summaries) to ~750k–1.79M Toman/year (Taaghche/Bookapo/Navaar subscriptions).

## 1. Size-signal proxies for the qualified segment (all proxies, labeled)

**Headline caveat:** every number below measures a *superset* of the qualified segment (digital readers, religious readers, summary-app users). None measures "tried digests, sensed they didn't learn, wants depth." The qualified fraction is unmeasured **[H — this is precisely why the interview falsifier exists]**.

### 1.1 Digital-reading population (Farsi, Iran)

- Taaghche (largest e-book/audiobook platform): **15M+ cumulative installs (1403 report)**, up from ~12M (1402); users who read at least one book grew 50% YoY in 1403; 130k+ titles. Self-reported. **[F, self-reported]** [S1][S2]
- Fidibo (Digikala-owned): **10M+ registered users** (self-reported via platform comparisons) [S3]; **~3M app installs with 1,180 publishers** (industry report) [S4]; 8,242 new titles added in 1403 [S5].
- Market-wide: **~3M users per month consume books digitally in Iran** (estimate quoted by a publisher/industry piece; methodology not published). **[F reported / weak estimate]** [S6]
- Navaar (audiobook + book-summary app, Taaghche-family): **1M+ users**, ~12k daily visits (Borna wire, undated). **[F, undated]** [S7]
- Cafe Bazaar: **50M monthly active users** (1403 report, self-published); Android ≈ **91% of Iran's device market**. **[F, self-reported]** [S8][S9]
- Myket: **50M active installs / 26M MAU / 1.5B downloads in 1403** (self-reported annual report). **[F, self-reported]** [S10]

*Proxy reading:* the reachable digital distribution base is effectively the whole Iranian smartphone population; the *paying-for-reading* base is plausibly in the 1–3M monthly-active band **[H]**, with no public split of free vs paying.

### 1.2 Religious/scholarly reading (beachhead-relevant)

- Religious books hold **~9% of Iran's publishing market, 5th-ranked category**, with a reported **76% YoY growth** in one recent year (IBNA). **[F]** [S11]
- Ten-year figures: **49,832 first-print religious titles + 53,078 reprints** — the reprint rate exceeding first prints signals sustained demand. **[F]** [S12]
- **جشنواره کتاب‌خوانی رضوی** (Razavi book-reading festival, Astan Quds Razavi / Imam Reza shrine, Mashhad): **9th edition 1.3M+ participants, 10th edition 1M+, 4M+ cumulative across editions** — participants read designated books and answer quizzes (written + electronic sections). **[F]** [S13][S14] This is the single strongest proxy for *engaged, whole-book religious readers who accept assessment* **[H]**.
- **حلقه‌های صالحین** (Salehin study circles, Basij-organized mosque network): **~340,000 active circles** (Rasa; earlier reports: 250,000 in 1393) [S15][S16]; circles run **15–20 members** each (Hamshahri) [S17] → arithmetic gives **~5–6M participants**. **[F circle counts; H on the multiplication — sizes and activity rates vary]** Politically/institutionally state-adjacent; reachable but with affiliation implications for positioning **[H]**.
- Public libraries (نهاد کتابخانه‌های عمومی): **~2,289 public libraries** (4,400+ service points), 50M volumes; membership claims conflict — **2.7M+ "active" members** (ISNA-reported 245% growth) vs **"10M+ members"** (secretary-general) vs a press critique alleging overcounting. **[F, conflicting — treat as a wide 2.7–10M band]** [S18][S19][S20]
- Overall reading rate: last official figure **12.2 min/day (1398 survey)**; contested range across sources 2–30 min/day; among actual readers 26.6 min/day vs 10.9 min/day across the whole population. **[F, contested methodology]** [S21][S22][S23] Low headline reading rate is the thesis's headwind *and* the reason digests dominate **[H]**; note religious reading (Quran, Mafatih) is sometimes excluded in the lowest estimates [S22].

### 1.3 The beachhead book itself: طرح کلی اندیشۀ اسلامی در قرآن

Correction captured during research: the book is by **Sayyid Ali Khamenei** — 28 lectures, Ramadan 1353 (1974), Mashhad — first printed 1354; **not** by Motahari. **[F]** [S24][S25]

- Official publisher **Sahba (مؤسسه صهبا)** sells print and **distributes the full PDF free**, plus full lecture audio and per-session audio summaries; free text/audio also on khamenei.ir's book archive and Eitaa channels; a full free web text exists at lib.eshia.ir. **[F]** [S26][S27][S28]
- Print edition (e.g., Sahba) retails around **55,000 Toman** (~$1 at open-market rates); at least **15 printings by 1374 (1995)** per the Gisoom bibliography; **cumulative print run not found** — stated as a gap. **[F]** [S25][S29]
- The book is present on commercial platforms: ebook on Taaghche [S30], audiobook (narrator Abbas Moghadamzadeh) on Fidibo [S31] — digital demand for this exact title exists alongside the free official copies **[F]**.

*Implication (stays with PM):* access to the text is already solved at zero price — monetizable value cannot be "the book"; it can only be the guided-learning layer. This aligns with the agent-as-teacher stance from #37. **[H]**

The two worlds overlap but are not identical; channel choice is a positioning decision for the PM. **[H]**

## 3. Discovery and payment: rails, app stores, pricing anchors

### 3.1 Payment rails (hard constraints)

- Iran's domestic interbank **SHETAB** system is closed to global card networks; **Visa/Mastercard/Amex do not work** in Iran (sanctions). **[F]** [S43][S44][S45]
- Consequence: no Google Play / App Store paid billing for Iranian users; Iranian apps monetize via **Cafe Bazaar / Myket in-app billing** (Rial/Toman, SHETAB-funded wallets) or **web payment gateways** (Zarinpal et al.) — the standard pattern for Navaar, Bookapo, Fidibo, Taaghche. **[F for the rails; H for "standard pattern" — inferred from each platform's domestic-only payment pages]**
- iOS reality: Apple devices ≈ 9% of the market [S9]; Iranian iOS users install via **Sibapp / Sibche / iApps / Anardooni** alternative stores (Sibapp claims ~70% of Iranian iOS alt-store share, self-claimed) — paid iOS distribution is via these stores or web PWA. **[F, self-claimed share]** [S46][S47]

### 3.3 Willingness to pay for depth vs digests — what the evidence does and doesn't say

- No public study, platform report, or survey measuring Iranian users' willingness to pay for *depth over digests* was found within search budget. **This is the honest gap; it is interview-only evidence.** 
- Indirect signals, all **[H]** interpretations over **[F]** facts:
  - A real paid market for summaries exists at 28k–149k T/month (Sabketo, Bookapo) and for audiobook subscriptions at ~750k–1.25M T/year (Taaghche/Navaar discounted street prices) [S48][S50][S51][S52] — the segment already pays for *reading-adjacent* digital products.
  - The pervasive 30–80% discount culture on every platform [S50][S51][S52][S54] suggests high price sensitivity / promotional dependence — a constraint on premium depth pricing at launch. 
  - Per-title audiobook purchases up to 352,000 T [S53] and single course purchases ~119k–278k T [S54] bound what one "deep single-work" product can charge per completion (call it 100k–350k T) **[H]**.
  - Free availability of the beachhead book's text and audio [S26][S27] means a paywall on content will read as illegitimate to this audience; value must be pedagogy — a fact that *sharpens* the depth-vs-digest test rather than softening it. **[H]**

## 4. What this implies for GTM options (brief; decisions stay with the PM)

1. **Sizing honesty:** qualified-segment size is unknowable from desk research. Bounding proxies: ~1–3M monthly digital readers [S1][S3][S6] (broad), 1M+/yr assessed religious readers (festival) [S13], ~5–6M institutional study-circle members (arithmetic) [S15][S17]. The interviews (#37 falsifier) are the only instrument that converts any of these into a qualified-segment estimate. 
2. **Channel candidates by audience fit:** (i) Razavi-festival / Salehin-adjacent religious-learning communities for the beachhead book's audience; (ii) Telegram/Eitaa book channels for reach (with piracy-culture friction); (iii) BPLUS-style summary-podcast audiences as the *dissatisfied-by-definition* pool — they already consume digests and are the natural "felt informed, learned nothing" population; (iv) Cafe Bazaar/Myket + PWA web (no Google/Apple billing) as the transactional surface. 
3. **Pricing posture:** anchors cluster at 100k–350k T for a single deep work/course and ~750k–1.8M T/year for library subscriptions; a per-book guided-learning product plausibly prices near the single-course band, with the Sabketo 28k T summary floor as the digest alternative it must visibly beat. 
4. **Content economics:** the beachhead text is free from the author's own institute — rights clearance for *learning over* the text (not redistributing it) should still be confirmed with Sahba; citing pages of a freely distributed text is low-risk but not zero-risk. **[H]**

## 5. Facts vs hypotheses ledger (summary)

- **[F] core:** publishing scale (116,754 titles 1403) [S55][S56]; book-fair 6M visits / 95B T 9-day sales [S40][S41]; platform self-reports (Taaghche 15M installs, Fidibo 10M registered, Navaar 1M+, Bazaar 50M MAU, Myket 26M MAU); religious-category ~9% share + reprint-heavy demand [S11][S12]; Razavi festival 1M+/yr [S13]; Salehin 340k circles × 15–20 members [S15][S17]; SHETAB isolation / no Visa-MC [S43][S44]; Bookapo/Taaghche/Sabketo prices from official pages; beachhead book authorship + free official PDF/audio + presence on Taaghche/Fidibo [S24][S26][S30][S31].
- **[H] core:** 5–6M Salehin participants (multiplication); ~1–3M monthly digital readers as the paying-relevant band; the qualified depth-dissatisfied fraction (unmeasurable from desk); piracy-culture friction in Telegram channels; pricing-band inference for the product; GTM channel fit.
- **Known-unknown / could not source:** Telegram/Instagram community member counts (platform-blocked from here); beachhead book cumulative print run; any direct WTP-for-depth data; Bookapo/Fidibo/Navaar paid-subscriber counts (as opposed to registered users).

## 6. Source-reliability notes

- Platform self-reports (Taaghche, Fidibo, Navaar, Bazaar, Myket, Sibapp) are marketing numbers: installs/registrations ≠ actives ≠ payers. Treated as ceilings.
- Official-price readings were taken 2026-10-02; Iranian e-commerce reprices frequently under inflation; discount-cycle prices are the effective market.
- Where the Farsi web could not be verified from this machine (Telegram member counts, Instagram follower counts), it is stated above rather than estimated.
